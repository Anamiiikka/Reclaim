### On diagnosis accuracy

Overall accuracy is 99.8%, and that number is not a result.
The generator writes a gateway failure code and the engine maps that same code
back through the same table, so agreement is arithmetic rather than inference.

The meaningful number is **92.5% on 40 genuinely ambiguous cases** --
unrecognised gateway codes where the engine has to fall back to heuristics.
The sample is small, and that is stated rather than hidden.

### Against a contact-everyone baseline

Restricted to the 932 cases both policies may act on unaided, so the
comparison isolates targeting rather than permission.

| | Reclaim | Contact everyone |
|---|---|---|
| Customers contacted | 781 | 932 |
| Recovered (simulated) | 325 | 358 |
| Revenue (simulated) | Rs 874,843 | Rs 985,230 |

**This is a trade-off, not a win.** Contacting everyone recovers
Rs 110,387 more (13%) by sending 151 more
messages (19%). Reclaim gives up some recovery to avoid
contacting people it judges unlikely to convert.

Whether that is the right call is a merchant's decision, not ours -- the floor
that produces it is a single configurable threshold. What Reclaim provides is
the ability to make that choice deliberately, and to see what it costs.

## Caveats

- Recovery outcomes are drawn from a documented response model, not observed.
- The control arm holds 151 cases; at that size the confidence interval on its rate is wide.
- Uplift should be read as directional under stated assumptions, not as a revenue forecast.
